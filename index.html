Wenn die verantwortliche Person ihr eigenes Pinterest-Konto verbindet, speichert das Programm lokal auf dem eigenen Computer die dafür nötigen Zugriffsschlüssel (Access- und Refresh-Token) sowie Namen der eigenen Pinterest-Boards. Alle Daten liegen in einer lokalen Datei auf dem Computer der verantwortlichen Person und können dort jederzeit gelöscht werden. Der Zugriff der App auf Pinterest lässt sich jederzeit in den Pinterest-Kontoeinstellungen widerrufen.

2. Scope

This application ("Affiliat") is a private tool that runs only on the computer of the responsible person. It has no public website, no accounts for third parties, no cookies and no visitor tracking.

3. Data processed

When the responsible person connects their own Pinterest account, the application stores the required access tokens and the names of their own boards locally on their own computer. It also stores Amazon product data (such as title, price and image reference) and self-written Pin drafts locally. No personal data of other users is collected.

4. Sharing

Data is sent to Pinterest only when the responsible person explicitly approves and publishes a Pin. Data is not shared with other third parties, sold or used for advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

All data is kept in a local file on the responsible person's computer and can be deleted there at any time. The app's access to Pinterest can be revoked at any time in the Pinterest account settings.
